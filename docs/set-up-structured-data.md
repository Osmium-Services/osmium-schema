**Osmium Schema** tells search engines who you are, in a form they can read. It describes your business, and can help your listing show address, opening hours and contact details.

## Set your business type

Open **Osmium Schema Settings**.

- **Type:** choose **Organization** for a company with no premises. The other types are LocalBusiness types, which also include your location and opening hours.
- Fill in the **Name**, **Legal name**, **Logo** and **Description**. Fields you leave empty use your site name and your branding details, shown in grey. A value you type wins.
- **Price range** is a rough guide for LocalBusiness types: either currency signs (£ to ££££) or a range such as £10-£50. Leave it empty if prices vary too much.

## Address, opening hours and map position

The address and opening hours come from your Company Location settings, so there is nothing to enter twice. Latitude and longitude are looked up from your postcode if you leave them empty. Coordinates you type are kept.

## Contact and social links

Empty **Email** and **Phone** use your branding details, and are added as a customer-service contact point. If you use Osmium Social Links, those profiles feed in too.

## Leave the Site URL alone

**Site URL** is used to build the identifiers. Leave it empty unless your domain changes.

Press **Save Settings** when you are done.
